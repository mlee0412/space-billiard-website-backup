FAQ

Quick answers before you book.

Can groups reserve ahead?

Yes. Email or call with the date, guest count, arrival time, and event style.

Is this good for private parties?

Yes. Space Billiard works well for birthdays, corporate groups, team nights, and social events.

What are your hours?

Open 7 days a week until 3 AM or later. Happy hour runs Sunday through Thursday until 7 PM.

Do I need a reservation?

No. Walk-ins are welcome anytime during normal business hours — tables are subject to availability on a first-come, first-serve basis.

Is smoking or vaping allowed?

No. The building has strict no smoking/vaping restrictions covering the pool table section, the bar, and the elevator.

Where is the venue?

Space Billiard is at 34 W 32nd St, Floor 12, in Koreatown. Get directions.
